# Draft spoken pitch — approximately 60 seconds

University students spend too much revision time scrubbing through lecture
recordings and searching separate slide decks. Studying in a second language
makes that even harder.

Our lecture assistant lets a student upload a recording and supporting PDFs,
then review a searchable transcript, ask a question about a selected moment,
and get an explanation in English or Italian. Answers link back to the original
video time or document page. The transcript can also be translated while the
video timeline stays intact.

We combine local Whisper transcription, local multilingual embeddings and
hybrid retrieval with a language model through OpenRouter. When our sample
material did not support a question, the assistant returned insufficient evidence.

The integrated prototype has passed automated tests and live checks. Next we
will validate longer recordings and student learning, and replace demo accounts
with real authentication.
